# Home — surface brief

Scope: home page (`/` and `/en`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: startup/SaaS founders and product leads (primary), PME owners (secondary), deciding whether to trust one senior engineer with an AI project. Action: open a project request (short form with budget range; calendar for qualified leads). Proof: live products measured in the browser, real commit history from the repos, Mercado Livre and CI&T, Spindow and Luvi cases. Constraints: no prices, no invented metrics or uptime history, no testimonials until received, Luvi shown as deactivated.

Anti-goals (user): must not read as an agency, an AI template, a junior portfolio, or corporate and cold.

Memorable moment: the systems list measures each live product from the visitor's own browser on load.

Unresolved: capacity wording, Cal.com hours, testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the status page of the systems Douglas keeps in production. Refuses the consultant hero-plus-service-cards page and the dark AI landing.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, one grotesk with tabular figures. Activity bars from real monthly commits are the only graphic motif. Green exists by law only for a state measured live; deactivated and in-construction are printed marks, never hues.

STORY: The visitor sees his systems answering now, believes this person ships things that stay up, reads the cases as incident notes, and opens a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name and "Abrir um projeto" in a plain top bar. H1 "Coloco IA dentro do seu produto." at display scale, left, supporting line beneath. Below, full width: the systems list with live response times and commit bars; the on-call line carries his portrait.
